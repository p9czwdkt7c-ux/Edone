# iPhone 16 Pro vs iPhone 17 Pro vs iPhone 18 Pro: Full Comparison

*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

This compares Apple's last three Pro generations, with the Pro Max models alongside:

| | iPhone 16 Pro / Pro Max | iPhone 17 Pro / Pro Max | iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Announced | 9 Sep 2024 | 9 Sep 2025 | 9 Sep 2026 |
| Released | 20 Sep 2024 | 19 Sep 2025 | 18 Sep 2026 |
| Launch OS | iOS 18 | iOS 26 | iOS 27 |
| Status (Oct 2026) | Discontinued by Apple | Discontinued by Apple (Pro line replaced) | Current |

> **About the sources.** The iPhone 16 Pro and 17 Pro figures are Apple's published specs. The iPhone 18 Pro figures come from Apple's spec and support pages, its September 2026 press materials, and launch-week coverage (MacRumors, AppleInsider, 9to5Mac, Engadget, PhoneArena, DPReview, Notebookcheck, Tom's Guide, GSMArena). Where sources disagree, both values are given and marked ⚠️. Apple's battery-life figures also differ by region and by SIM variant: eSIM-only models (e.g. US) hold a larger battery.

## 1. TL;DR

| If you have… | Upgrade to 18 Pro? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **iPhone 16 Pro** | **Yes, it's a big jump.** | +4 GB RAM, 2nm chip with ~35–40% more CPU and GPU speed, a vapor chamber, a 48MP 4× telephoto (replacing the 12MP 5×), an 18MP Center Stage selfie camera, a 3000-nit outdoor display, 60W-class charging, a variable-aperture main camera, and roughly 8–12 extra hours of video playback. |
| **iPhone 17 Pro** | **Probably not.** | Same design, display, RAM, telephoto and ultrawide. You'd gain the variable aperture, a smaller Dynamic Island, a faster chip and GPU, a bigger vapor chamber, ~3 h more battery, faster wired charging and a 2TB option on the small Pro. It costs **$100 more** and weighs ~5 g more. |
| **Buying fresh** | **18 Pro** if budget allows. Otherwise a discounted or refurbished **17 Pro** is the value pick, since it has most of the hardware. |

**The three generations in one line each**

- **16 Pro (2024):** last titanium Pro, first with Camera Control, first 48MP ultrawide, 5× telephoto on both sizes, 4K120 Dolby Vision. 8 GB RAM.
- **17 Pro (2025):** biggest redesign since the iPhone X: aluminum unibody, full-width camera "plateau", vapor chamber, 12 GB RAM, three 48MP rear cameras, 18MP square-sensor selfie camera, N1 wireless chip, much bigger battery.
- **18 Pro (2026):** refinement year. 2nm A20 Pro, **variable-aperture main camera (ƒ/1.48–ƒ/4)**, ~35% narrower Dynamic Island (Face ID IR camera under the display), 3× larger vapor chamber, Apple C2 modem (most models), 60W fast charging, 2TB on both sizes. Base price up $100.

## 5. Chip, memory & performance

| | A18 Pro (16 Pro) | A19 Pro (17 Pro) | A20 Pro (18 Pro) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Process | TSMC 3nm (N3E) | TSMC 3nm (N3P) | **TSMC 2nm (N2)**, Apple's first |
| CPU | 6-core (2P + 4E) | 6-core (2P + 4E) | 6-core |
| GPU | 6-core | 6-core with **Neural Accelerators in each core** | **7-core** ⚠️ (as reported); Apple says up to ~40% faster than A19 Pro |
| Neural Engine | 16-core | 16-core | 16-core plus upgraded on-device AI hardware |
| RAM | **8 GB** | **12 GB** | **12 GB** (confirmed via Xcode; a 16 GB plan was reportedly dropped) |
| Cooling | No vapor chamber | Vapor chamber | Vapor chamber with ~3× the area |

### Benchmarks (approximate; reviews and databases vary)

| | 16 Pro | 17 Pro | 18 Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geekbench 6 single-core | ~3,400 | ~3,700–3,800 | **~4,700** (Tom's Guide: 4,735) |
| Geekbench 6 multi-core | ~8,500 | ~9,500–10,000 | **~12,800** (Tom's Guide: 12,800) |
| 3DMark Solar Bay Unlimited | — | — | 16,228 (61.9 fps avg); Pro Max 67.9 fps |

**Notes**
- The 16 → 18 jump is roughly **+35–40% single-core and +50% multi-core**. The GPU and AI gains are bigger, helped by the extra RAM and the Neural Accelerators added in the A19 Pro.
- Under sustained load (gaming, long 4K/ProRes recording) the 16 Pro throttles the most. Reviewers say the 17 Pro's vapor chamber fixed most of that. On the 18 Pro, Trusted Reviews saw steady performance over long runs, while Notebookcheck found the larger chamber makes little difference in everyday use.
- All three support Apple Intelligence. 8 GB is Apple's minimum, so the 16 Pro has the least headroom for future on-device models. The 12 GB models will age better.

## 6. Cameras

### Rear camera hardware

| | 16 Pro / Pro Max | 17 Pro / Pro Max | 18 Pro / Pro Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Main** | 48MP Fusion, 24 mm, ƒ/1.78, 2nd-gen sensor-shift OIS | 48MP Fusion, 24 mm, ƒ/1.78, 2nd-gen sensor-shift | **48MP Fusion, 24 mm, variable aperture ƒ/1.48 – ƒ/4.0** (stops at ƒ/1.48, ƒ/1.8, ƒ/2.8, ƒ/4; six laser-cut blades), 2nd-gen sensor-shift |
| **Ultra Wide** | 48MP, 13 mm, ƒ/2.2, 120° | 48MP, 13 mm, ƒ/2.2, 120° | 48MP, 13 mm, ƒ/2.2, 120° (same module) |
| **Telephoto** | **12MP 5× tetraprism, 120 mm, ƒ/2.8** | **48MP 4× tetraprism, 100 mm, ƒ/2.8**, 56% larger sensor, 3D sensor-shift | 48MP 4× tetraprism, 100 mm, ƒ/2.8 (carried over from 17 Pro) |
| Optical-quality zoom stops | 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 5× | 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4×, **8×** | 0.5×, 1×, 2×, 4×, 8× |
| Optical zoom range | 10× | **16×** | 16× |
| Max digital zoom | 25× | **40×** | 40× |
| Rear sensors that are 48MP | 2 of 3 | **3 of 3** | 3 of 3 |

**What the variable aperture does on the 18 Pro**
- **ƒ/1.48** lets in more light than any previous iPhone main camera, which helps in low light and gives shallower natural background blur.
- **ƒ/2.8 – ƒ/4** gives more depth of field for group shots, close-ups, landscapes and food. Stopping down also shows sharp "sunstars" from point lights, and in bright light it extends usable shutter speeds.
- The new **Pro controls** in the Camera app give manual aperture, shutter speed and white balance.

**On the 16 Pro → 17 Pro telephoto swap:** the 16 Pro's 5× (120 mm) reaches further optically. The 17/18 Pro's 4× uses a 48MP sensor, so its 8× (200 mm) crop has more detail and the module does far better in low light. For most people it's an upgrade. If you mostly shoot at exactly 5×, it's a sidegrade.

## 9. Software & longevity

- All three run **iOS 27**, including Apple Intelligence and the new Siri, which rolled out in beta with iOS 27.
- Apple usually supports iPhones for 6–7+ years of iOS updates. Rough end-of-support:
  - **16 Pro**: around 2030–31, with the least AI headroom (8 GB RAM).
  - **17 Pro**: around 2031–32.
  - **18 Pro**: around 2032–33.
- Camera features that need hardware won't come to older models: the variable aperture is 18 Pro only. ProRes RAW, Genlock, Dual Capture and the Center Stage front camera need a 17 Pro or later.

## 10. Everything that changed, generation by generation

### iPhone 16 Pro → iPhone 17 Pro (2025)
1. Titanium → **aluminum unibody** with a full-width camera plateau
2. **Vapor-chamber cooling** added
3. A18 Pro → **A19 Pro** (N3P, Neural Accelerators in the GPU)
4. **8 GB → 12 GB RAM**
5. 12MP 5× telephoto → **48MP 4× telephoto** with 8× optical-quality zoom, 40× digital
6. 12MP front → **18MP Center Stage** square sensor
7. 2000 → **3000-nit** outdoor brightness, **Ceramic Shield 2** with anti-reflective coating
8. Much larger battery: **+4–6 h** video playback
9. **40 W-class** wired fast charging (50% in 20 min), Qi2.2
10. **N1** wireless chip (Bluetooth 6) and Qualcomm X80
11. ProRes RAW, Apple Log 2, Genlock, Dual Capture
12. Base storage 128 → **256 GB**, 2 TB option on Pro Max
13. US models go **eSIM-only**
14. +7 g (Pro) and +6 g (Pro Max)

### iPhone 17 Pro → iPhone 18 Pro (2026)
1. A19 Pro → **A20 Pro, 2nm**: about +25% single-core and +30% multi-core in Geekbench 6, GPU up to ~40% faster
2. **Variable-aperture main camera** (ƒ/1.48 – ƒ/4) and new **Pro controls** for manual shooting
3. **~35% narrower Dynamic Island** (Face ID IR camera under the display)
4. Vapor chamber with **~3× the surface area**
5. **Apple C2 modem** on most models
6. **60 W-class** wired charging (50% in ~15 min)
7. More battery: **+3 h (Pro) and +4–6 h (Pro Max)** video playback
8. **2 TB** option on the 6.3" Pro
9. 4K Dolby Vision time-lapse, 4K60 Cinematic mode
10. Glass inset colour-matched to the aluminum; new colours (Black, Silver, Glacier, Burgundy)
11. **+$100** base price; +5 g (Pro) and +16 g (Pro Max)
12. **Unchanged:** size, display panel, RAM (12 GB), ultrawide, telephoto, front camera, MagSafe wattage, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth (N1), USB speeds, IP68

### iPhone 16 Pro → iPhone 18 Pro (two-generation jump)
Everything in both lists above. The biggest real-world differences:
- **Battery:** about 9–12 more hours of video, plus 50% in ~15 min instead of ~30
- **Telephoto:** 12MP → 48MP, with a usable 8× crop
- **Sustained performance:** no cooling → a large vapor chamber, and a ~40% faster CPU
- **RAM:** 8 GB → 12 GB, which matters for Apple Intelligence over the coming years
- **Camera control:** variable aperture and manual Pro controls
- **Trade-offs:** heavier (+12 g Pro, +22 g Pro Max), aluminum instead of titanium, and $200 more at the base (though the base model now has 256 GB)

## 11. Which one should you buy?

| Priority | Best pick |
|---|---|
| Best overall, longest support | **iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max** |
| Best value (refurb/used, Oct 2026) | **iPhone 17 Pro**: most of the 18 Pro's hardware for much less |
| Lightest, most compact Pro | **iPhone 16 Pro** (199 g), with titanium |
| Longest battery | **iPhone 18 Pro Max** |
| Photography enthusiasts | **iPhone 18 Pro**, for the variable aperture and Pro controls |
| Longer optical reach (5× native) | **iPhone 16 Pro** has the longest native focal length (120 mm); the 17/18 Pro's 8× crop reaches further at good quality |
| Pro video (ProRes RAW, Genlock) | 17 Pro or 18 Pro |
| Gaming / sustained performance | **iPhone 18 Pro Max** (largest vapor chamber, fastest GPU) |
| Apple-designed modem | 18 Pro (any region), or 18 Pro Max outside the US |
